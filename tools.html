Will this file play in my show?

Find out now, not in tech. This is the question behind most "it worked on my laptop" disasters — a file that plays fine in one program and refuses to open in the browser.

Trim a video

Most clips arrive with a few seconds of nothing at each end — a logo, a countdown, the moment someone reaches for the camera. Cut them off here and the cue starts where you want it to.

Drop a video here, or click to choose Nothing is uploaded. It is trimmed on this computer.

Make a video loop endlessly

A background that has to hold for a whole scene — flames, rain, drifting cloud — gives itself away with a jump every time it restarts. This blends the end back over the beginning, so there is no join to see.

Drop a video here, or click to choose Nothing is uploaded. It is made on this computer.

Play the video, find a moment you are happy to start and end on, and cut there — or just drag the marker. Everything after the cut plays first, everything before it plays second, and the two original ends meet in the middle where the blend hides them.

Make a title card

A slide that says Act Two, or Intermission — 15 minutes, or nothing at all in pure black. Type it, and you get a picture at exactly the size your projector wants.

Press Enter for a new line. Leave it empty for a plain colour card.

Get the sound out of a video

Sometimes the effect you need only exists inside a video — a clip off a phone, a song under a trailer. This lifts the sound track out whole and hands it back as an audio file you can drop straight onto a cue.

Drop a video here, or click to choose Nothing is uploaded. The sound is pulled out on this computer.

Shrink a video

A 4K clip will often play on your laptop and then stutter through the projector. Re-encoding it near the size it is actually shown at fixes that, and nobody in the audience can tell.

Drop a video here, or click to choose Nothing is uploaded. It is encoded on this computer.
