Trim a video

Most clips arrive with a few seconds of nothing at each end — a logo, a countdown, the moment someone reaches for the camera. Cut them off here and the cue starts where you want it to.

Drop a video here, or click to choose Nothing is uploaded. It is trimmed on this computer.

Play the video, and when you reach the spot you want, press one of those buttons. You can also drag the two handles on the bar.

Shrink a video

A 4K clip will often play on your laptop and then stutter through the projector. Re-encoding it near the size it is actually shown at fixes that, and nobody in the audience can tell.

Drop a video here, or click to choose Nothing is uploaded. It is encoded on this computer.
