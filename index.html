Product Highlights

The complete educational audio solution.
Professional-grade show control designed specifically for student operators and Chromebook environments.

Show Control

  • Unified Workflow: Fade All, Stop All, and instant "Go" playback.
  • Multi-Layer Playback: Seamless overlapping audio instances.
  • Automation: Auto-Follow, Auto-Continue, and Infinite Loops.
  • Hotkeys: Instant-fire sound effects grid (1-0).
  • Waveform Clarity: Hatched trim zones; distinct green/red markers.
  • High Contrast Mode: Bold waveform colors for projection.
  • Oversize Transport: Projection-visible GO/STOP/FADE controls.

Management

  • Export Center: Generate printable PDF Cue Sheets and portable Zip packages.
  • Load Project Zip: Open an entire show (cue list and audio together) in one step.
  • Waveform Editor: Visual non-destructive trimming and fading.
  • Fully Offline: Every feature runs on the device, exports included (perfect for air-gapped booths).
  • Student Safe: "Help Mode" overlay preventing accidental edits.
  • Multi-Folder Pool: Multiple audio folders in one pool.
  • Consolidate to Folder: Package show and audio into one folder.
  • Audio Search: Filter audio files in real time.
Theatre Cue Player Extension
In testing — not yet on the Chrome Web Store

A Chrome extension that adds video and projection to everything the browser version already does. It is being tested in real productions now and will be published to the Chrome Web Store once that testing is finished. Audio-only shows will always be free.

Video & Projection

  • Second-Screen Output: Clean full-screen projection to a projector or second monitor, black between cues.
  • Projection Mapping: Corner-pin warp and masking onto set pieces, scrims, and odd-shaped surfaces.
  • Video Cues: Fades, loops, trimming, playback rate, and pause/resume, fired from the same cue list as your audio.
  • Text Cues: Titles, supertitles, crawls and rolls.

Shows That Travel

  • Two Storage Modes: A plain folder on the machine, or your own Google Drive — your choice, changeable at any time.
  • Cue Sheet Export: Generate a formatted Google Sheet of your cue list for the stage manager and the booth.
  • Built for Chromebooks: Runs offline once a show is open, so a dropped network can't stop a performance.
  • Sound Editor: Trim, fade, and clean up audio without leaving the app.

How it uses Google Drive

Google Drive is optional. The extension works completely without it — in folder mode it never contacts Google, never asks you to sign in, and makes no network connections at all, which matters in districts where Drive is blocked.

If you do connect it, shows are saved to your own Drive so a cue list follows you from the classroom to the booth, and cue sheets can be exported straight to Google Sheets. It asks for a single per-file permission (drive.file) which lets it read and write only the show files it creates and the cue sheets it makes for you. It cannot see anything else in your Drive, and there is no Theatre Cue Player server — nothing you make is sent to the developer. Full detail is in the privacy policy.

Roadmap

  • Extension (in testing): Video cues, projection mapping, second-screen output, and optional Google Drive storage.
  • Future Development: MIDI and OSC support.
